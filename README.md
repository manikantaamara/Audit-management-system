# 🚀 AuditFlow – Audit Information Management System

AuditFlow is a web-based Audit Information Management System developed to simplify and digitize the audit process within an organization. It provides role-based access, audit planning, team management, report generation, and audit tracking through an intuitive dashboard.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Traditional audit processes often rely on manual documentation and communication, making them time-consuming and difficult to manage. AuditFlow centralizes the entire audit lifecycle—from audit planning to report submission—into a secure digital platform.

The system enables administrators, audit heads, team leaders, auditors, and departments to collaborate efficiently while maintaining transparency and accountability.

## 👥 User Roles

### 1. Head of Department (HOD)
- Manage audit teams
- Assign Group Leaders
- Schedule audits
- Monitor overall audit progress
- View reports and analytics

### 2. Group Leader
- Manage assigned auditors
- Allocate audit tasks
- Track team progress
- Review submitted reports

### 3. Auditor
- View assigned audits
- Submit observations
- Upload audit reports
- Update audit status

### 4. Department
- View assigned audits
- Respond to audit findings
- Upload compliance documents
- Track audit completion
